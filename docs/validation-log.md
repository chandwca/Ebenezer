## Assumptions worth trying to break

1. Students want Scripture in their own language, not only English.
2. A link from a ministry leader is opened as readily as one from a friend.
3. Prayer wording is welcome; some students may prefer "wisdom" or "story".
4. Sharing religious content in a group chat is safe for the people receiving it.
5. A one-tap reply is easier than writing a message; a journal is wanted at all.
6. The seven moments match real moments; the passages fit them.
7. A five-day pack is more useful than a single card, and five days is the right length.
8. Hearing the verse read aloud helps; a printed QR at an orientation table would actually be scanned.

## People to ask

- A campus ministry or international-student-office contact.
- Three to five students from different regions and backgrounds, including at least one not from a Christian background.
- A native-speaker reviewer for each interface or Scripture language shown.
